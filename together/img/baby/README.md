# What baby looks like (illustrations)

`4.webp` … `40.webp` show under the fruit on Today and in Week by week
(weeks 41–42 reuse 40). A week without a file shows just the fruit.

`make_pictures.py` makes them with the OpenAI image API, one medically
detailed prompt per week: `python3 make_pictures.py 4 5 6 … 40`, then convert
the PNGs to 800×800 WebP. They're illustrations, not photos, and are on the
expert review checklist.
